Step-by-Step OTP Fixes

  1. Verify Number Format: Double-check that your 10-digit mobile number is entered properly without extra spaces or wrong digits.
  2. Check Network & Inbox: Toggle Airplane mode on for 10 seconds and off, check cellular reception, and check your SMS Spam/Blocked messages folder.
  3. Wait for Cooldown Timer: Tapping resend repeatedly can trigger temporary rate limits. Wait for the timer to finish before requesting a new OTP.
  4. Check Storage & App Version: Ensure your phone has free space and you are running the latest APK build over a stable connection.

Dual SIM or DND Issues

Make sure the SIM receiving SMS messages has active network service and isn't blocking promotional/service SMS senders.
